Dzana - Afrobeats Dance Group

Choreographer | Creative Lead

As a choreographer for Dzana, an Afrobeats dance group on Compass at Yale, I combine creativity with leadership to:

  • Create & Teach: Design original choreography and teach routines to group members
  • Lead Rehearsals: Organize and direct practice sessions, ensuring everyone learns and improves
  • Cultural Expression: Share African culture and Afrobeats music through dance performances
  • Build Community: Foster an inclusive environment where members can express themselves
  • Performance Planning: Coordinate group performances for campus events and showcases

What Choreography Teaches Me: Breaking down complex movements into teachable steps parallels how I approach engineering problems — analyzing systems, understanding components, and creating structured solutions. The creativity and expression in dance balance the analytical rigor of engineering.
